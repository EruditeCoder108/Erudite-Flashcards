Privacy Commitment

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Erudite Systems

1. Local-First Architecture

Erudite Flashcards is designed around a local-first data philosophy. All your flashcards, decks, image occlusion masks, audio clips, review history, FSRS algorithm state variables, and personal settings are created and saved directly on your Android device in a local SQLite database.

We do not operate central servers that log or track your daily study habits, flashcard content, or performance metrics.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We collect zero personal identity data. Specifically:

  • No User Account: You do not need to register with an email, phone number, or social login to use Erudite Flashcards.
  • No Content Tracking: Your flashcards and study notes remain strictly on your device. We cannot read, analyze, or monetize your study content.
  • No Analytics Tracking: We do not embed third-party advertising or invasive telemetry SDKs in the application.

3. Network Connections

Erudite Flashcards operates completely offline. Network access is utilized strictly for optional, user-initiated actions:

  • Downloading Premade Decks: When you choose to download free NCERT or community study volumes, the app fetches compressed deck files directly from our public content distribution endpoint.
  • External AI Integration: If you use the AI prompt workspace, the app helps you compile formatted prompts for external services (such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini). Any interaction with those external AI tools is governed by their respective privacy policies.

4. Data Backup and Portability

Because your data resides on your device, you are in complete control of your backups. Erudite Flashcards includes built-in full export tools that output standard JSON formats and media archives. You can move your backups to local storage, SD cards, cloud drives, or transfer them to another device at any time.

5. Data Deletion

Uninstalling Erudite Flashcards or clearing the app storage via your Android system settings immediately deletes all local database tables, decks, and media files from your device.
