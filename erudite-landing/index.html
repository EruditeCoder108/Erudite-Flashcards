Got questions?

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about Erudite Flashcards, FSRS scheduling, local data privacy, and Android installation.

Yes. Erudite Flashcards is completely free to use with full card creation, image occlusion tools, analytics, and all 31 NCERT Biology chapter decks without subscription fees or paywalls.

Erudite incorporates the Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler (FSRS 5.0). Unlike legacy SM-2 algorithms, FSRS dynamically calculates review intervals based on your custom target retention rate (between 80% and 95%) and memory stability curve.

Yes. Erudite is a local-first application. Your flashcards, media files, and study logs remain stored in a local SQLite database on your device. Internet connection is used only for downloading optional premade decks or external AI prompts.

Yes. You can export complete portable JSON backups of your decks and media files whenever you want to back up or move your study library to another Android device.

Erudite supports Basic (with linked reverse cards), Cloze Deletion, Image Occlusion (rectangular & oval masks), KaTeX math formulas, audio samples, and rich HTML tables.
