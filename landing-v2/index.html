Cognitive Memory Science

Why FSRS 5.0 Beats Legacy SM-2 Algorithms

Erudite uses the Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler (FSRS 5.0), mathematically tracking your memory stability ($S$), difficulty ($D$), and retrievability ($R$) to cut unnecessary reviews by up to 40%.

Scientific Retention Principles

Unlike legacy Leitner or SM-2 algorithms that rely on static multipliers, FSRS continuously calibrates review intervals to match your personal memory retention target (e.g. 90%).

Active Recall Memory Triggers

Forcing the brain to retrieve answers creates deeper neural pathways than passive textbook re-reading.

Anti-Burnout Fuzzing

Applies minor mathematical offsets to scheduled review dates, preventing card review spikes and study fatigue.

Leech Detection & Deck Health

Automatically identifies problem cards failed repeatedly, allowing you to refine prompts or split complex concepts.
