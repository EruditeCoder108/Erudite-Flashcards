Privacy & your data

Erudite Flashcards is published by Erudite Systems and keeps your study library on this device. You stay in control of exports, external services, and deleting your data.

What is stored on your phone

Erudite stores the flashcards you create or import, attached images and audio, classes, review history, study progress, app preferences, and local recovery snapshots. The app uses private app storage for its database and media.

When you export a backup, it is saved in the Erudite Flashcards backup folder in your device Documents storage so you can share or keep it. You control where that export goes after sharing it.

When the app connects to the internet

Erudite can download the optional premade decks you select from Erudite's deck host. It does not upload your personal decks, cards, images, or study history to that host.

If you choose an AI provider in the AI Deck Maker, Erudite opens that provider. Any document or study material you then submit is handled under that provider's own privacy terms and account settings. Avoid sharing highly sensitive material.

What Erudite does not collect

Erudite does not require sign-in, does not run advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not collect your card content, names, or study activity for the developer. Performance diagnostics remain on your device and intentionally omit card content.

Security and retention

App data stays in Android's private app storage and network requests use HTTPS. Local data remains until you delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Exported backups remain wherever you save or share them until you delete them, and they should be protected like any other study document.

The premade-deck host may receive standard request information such as an IP address, browser or app user agent, and request time for delivery, security, and abuse prevention. Erudite does not send card content or study history with those requests and does not maintain a separate user-profile database from them. External AI providers retain data according to their own terms and your account settings.

Your controls

  • Use Export Backup before changing devices or removing the app.
  • Use Delete All Local Data in the More tab to erase Erudite data, media, app-data backups, and settings from this device.
  • Delete any backup files you exported from the Files app or Documents folder when you no longer need them.
